Nexus is an offline-first study tracker for organizing courses, subjects, topics and review sessions.

Features:
• Organize streams, subjects and topics.
• Schedule reviews with the FSRS-6 spaced-repetition algorithm.
• Record review history and track topic progress.
• Use the Study Hub for planning, study logs, papers and goals.
• Explore and install supported curriculum packs.
• Review analytics and export records as CSV.
• Create and restore full JSON backups.

F-Droid Android edition:
• Works offline without creating an account.
• Stores study data locally on the device.
• Saves JSON backups and CSV exports to Downloads/Nexus.
• Uses the Android document picker to restore backups and import files.
• Does not include cloud synchronization or analytics.

Nexus is independently maintained and free to use.
