Account and Data Deletion

Last updated: 3 November 2026

You can request deletion of your Nexus cloud account and the study workspace associated with it. Account deletion is currently processed manually by the Nexus maintainer; there is not yet an automated in-app deletion control.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email from the email address associated with your Nexus sign-in account, where possible.
  2. Use the subject line Nexus account deletion request.
  3. Include the account email address you use with Nexus. Do not send your password, sign-in link, access token or other credentials.
  4. The maintainer may ask you to verify account ownership before processing the request.

Email a deletion request

What deletion covers

  • The Nexus Supabase authentication account associated with the verified request.
  • The cloud workspace data associated with that account in the Nexus database.

What deletion does not automatically remove

  • Study data stored locally in a browser or installed PWA on any device. Before or after the cloud request, open Nexus on each device and use its local workspace reset controls, or clear the browser's site data for the Nexus origin if you want those local copies removed. Export a backup first if you want to keep a copy.
  • JSON or CSV backups you previously downloaded, copied to another location or stored in a separate backup service. You must delete those copies yourself.
  • Technical records that a hosting or infrastructure provider retains under its own policies or where retention is required by law.

Before you request deletion

Cloud account deletion is intended to be permanent. Download a full JSON backup first if you want to retain your study records. Signing out is not the same as deleting an account. Resetting the local workspace is not the same as deleting cloud account data.
