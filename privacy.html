Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 November 2026

This policy explains how Nexus handles information when you use the Nexus Progressive Web App (PWA), including its offline study tools and optional cloud synchronization.

1. Information you create

Nexus is designed to work offline. Information you enter can include study streams, subjects, topics, notes, review ratings and dates, review history, exams, study plans, journals, past-paper records, goals, study-time logs, preferences and scheduler data. This information is your workspace content.

By default, your workspace is saved on the device and browser where you use Nexus. Nexus uses browser storage, including IndexedDB and a compatibility layer for existing local data. Browser authentication sessions may also be stored by the sign-in provider's client library.

2. Optional account and cloud synchronization

You can use Nexus without signing in. If you choose Google sign-in or email magic-link sign-in, Nexus uses Supabase authentication to establish your account session. Supabase receives the information needed to authenticate you, which can include your email address and provider account identifier.

When you are signed in and cloud synchronization is available, Nexus sends a workspace snapshot to the Nexus Supabase project so that the same account can access its study data on other devices. The cloud workspace includes the study and Study Hub records described above, together with relevant preferences and scheduler state. The cloud record is associated with the authenticated account. Account isolation and access controls are enforced by the application's Supabase configuration.

Cloud synchronization is optional. Signing out stops the signed-in session; it does not itself delete the local workspace or request deletion of the cloud account. Keep your own JSON backups for recovery.

3. Service providers and external services

  • Supabase: provides authentication and optional cloud database synchronization. Supabase processes data under its own terms and privacy documentation: Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • Google: if you choose Google sign-in, Google participates in the authentication process under Google's privacy terms: Google Privacy Policy.
  • Vercel: hosts the public Nexus web application. Hosting infrastructure may process technical request information such as IP address, browser information and request logs to deliver and secure the site: Vercel Privacy Policy.
  • jsDelivr: the app loads the Supabase JavaScript client library from this public CDN when cloud features are initialized. The CDN may process ordinary network request metadata: jsDelivr Privacy Policy.

Nexus does not currently include in-app advertising or a third-party behavioural advertising SDK. The application does not intentionally send your study content to an analytics service. If you manually enable the optional sync diagnostic mode, Nexus stores a limited diagnostic summary in your browser and provides a manual download; it is not automatically uploaded as part of that diagnostic feature.

4. Sharing and disclosure

Nexus does not sell your study workspace content. Workspace data is shared with the cloud service only when you choose to sign in and use cloud synchronization. Service providers may process technical information as described above to operate their services. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or to protect the security of the service.

5. Retention and your choices

  • Local workspace: remains in your browser/device until you reset it, clear the site's browser storage, or otherwise remove it. Uninstalling a PWA may not remove all browser-origin data on every device.
  • Downloaded backups: JSON/CSV files you export are saved under your control. Nexus cannot delete copies stored in your downloads, other folders, backups or other devices.
  • Cloud workspace and account: remain in the cloud account until you request account deletion and the request is verified and processed.
  • Hosting and provider logs: may be retained by the relevant infrastructure provider under its own policies and operational requirements.

You can use the in-app backup and restore controls to export or manage your workspace. To request deletion of your Nexus cloud account and associated cloud workspace, follow the instructions on the Account and Data Deletion page. A deletion request is currently handled manually; there is no in-app one-tap account deletion control. Deleting the cloud account does not automatically erase copies already stored locally on your devices or in files you exported.

6. Security

Nexus uses HTTPS for its hosted application and cloud connections. Cloud access is intended to be restricted to the authenticated account owner through Supabase access controls. No internet-connected service or local device can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect your device, email account and downloaded backups, and do not share sign-in links or credentials.

7. Children's privacy

Nexus is a general-purpose study tool and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children through a separate child-directed service. If you believe a child has provided personal information through an account, contact the support address below so the request can be reviewed.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Nexus features, infrastructure or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top will be updated when a revised policy is published.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, data requests or account-deletion requests, contact nexusorg.contact01@gmail.com.
